## About SEATrax

SEATrax is a blockchain platform that transforms trade finance by tokenizing shipping invoices as NFTs and enabling decentralized funding through investor pools. Exporters gain immediate access to liquidity (up to 70-100% advance funding), while investors earn competitive returns (4% yield) on verified trade finance opportunities.

The platform leverages smart contracts on Lisk Sepolia to automate fund distribution, payment verification, and profit sharing—creating a transparent, efficient, and trustless ecosystem for global trade finance.
